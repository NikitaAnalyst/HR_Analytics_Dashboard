# HR_Analytics_Dashboard
This HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of employee attrition within an organization, offering insights across multiple dimensions
## Key Metrics:
Total Employees: 882 <br>
Attrition Count: 150 <br>
Attrition Rate: 17% <br>
Average Age: 37 <br>
Average Salary: $6.4K <br>
Average Tenure: 6.9 years <br>

## Attrition Analysis:
By Education Field: Highest attrition is seen in Life Sciences and Medical fields. <br>
By Age Group: Employees aged 26–35 show the highest attrition (29.54%), followed by 36–45. <br>
By Gender: Attrition is nearly equal among Male and Female employees. <br>
By Job Role: Most attrition is seen among Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, and Research Scientists. <br>
By Years at Company: Highest attrition occurs within the first few years, especially around year 1–3. <br>
By Salary Bin: Lower salary brackets (especially up to $5k) have significantly higher attrition rates. <br>

## Department Breakdown:
Attrition is distributed across Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales departments. <br>
The accompanying matrix breaks down attrition across job roles by an unspecified categorization (1–4), possibly performance or tenure levels. <br>

## Why This Project Stands Out
This HR Analytics Dashboard stands out as a powerful, insight-driven tool for understanding employee attrition. It goes beyond static reports to deliver an interactive experience that empowers HR professionals and decision-makers to: <br>
Identify high-risk employee groups through age, salary, and tenure trends <br>
Analyze attrition by department, role, and education field <br>
Make data-informed retention strategies <br>
Its clean layout, intuitive visualizations, and actionable insights make it a valuable asset for both HR teams and business leadership. <br>
