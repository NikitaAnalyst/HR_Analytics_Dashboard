# HR_Analytics_Dashboard
This HR Analytics Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of employee attrition within an organization, offering insights across multiple dimensions
## Key Metrics:
Total Employees: 882
Attrition Count: 150
Attrition Rate: 17%
Average Age: 37
Average Salary: $6.4K
Average Tenure: 6.9 years

## Attrition Analysis:
By Education Field: Highest attrition is seen in Life Sciences and Medical fields.
By Age Group: Employees aged 26–35 show the highest attrition (29.54%), followed by 36–45.
By Gender: Attrition is nearly equal among Male and Female employees.
By Job Role: Most attrition is seen among Laboratory Technicians, Sales Executives, and Research Scientists.
By Years at Company: Highest attrition occurs within the first few years, especially around year 1–3.
By Salary Bin: Lower salary brackets (especially up to $5k) have significantly higher attrition rates.

## Department Breakdown:
Attrition is distributed across Human Resources, Research & Development, and Sales departments.
The accompanying matrix breaks down attrition across job roles by an unspecified categorization (1–4), possibly performance or tenure levels.

## Why This Project Stands Out
This HR Analytics Dashboard stands out as a powerful, insight-driven tool for understanding employee attrition. It goes beyond static reports to deliver an interactive experience that empowers HR professionals and decision-makers to:
Identify high-risk employee groups through age, salary, and tenure trends
Analyze attrition by department, role, and education field
Make data-informed retention strategies
Its clean layout, intuitive visualizations, and actionable insights make it a valuable asset for both HR teams and business leadership.

Attrition by Age Group, Gender, Education Field, Job Role, Salary Bin, and Years at Company
Department-level attrition overview and role-specific breakdowns
